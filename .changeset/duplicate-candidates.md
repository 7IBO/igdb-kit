`duplicateCandidates()` and `duplicateFields()` in `igdb-kit/game`: pairs of games that are likely one game entered twice in IGDB, or a port entered as a game of its own, each with a score from 0 to 1, its reasons and its conflicts. Editions, DLCs and other content, remakes and remasters next to their original, and games IGDB already links are left out.
